What does a stricter alarm buy?

Move the threshold and watch false alarms and one-year detection sensitivity fall together. Change how rare offenders are and the detector stays the same—but the odds behind an alarm move sharply.

The base-rate slider leaves these probabilities unchanged. It changes how often an offender is present, not how the chart behaves when one is present.

Read this as a scenario, not a case probability. “Detection” means the unit alarms in an offender-year, even if background deaths would have caused that alarm. It does not mean the offender is identified. The strictest points are noisier Monte Carlo estimates; the rarest target rests on roughly 50 calibrated crossings.
